Merge Birds is a calm little tapper about raising a backyard full of birds and turning their feathers into a small fortune. Start with two plain birds, drag one onto the other, and they merge into a better bird. Keep merging and your flock climbs from the OG bird into rare, strange, and frankly ridiculous species. Every bird sits around preening and dropping feathers, and those feathers become coins you spend on more birds. Your birds even keep working when you close the app, so there's a pile of feathers waiting whenever you come back.

As your backyard fills up, you unlock new places to keep birds, each worth more than the last: the Backyard, the Farm, the Warzone, the Beach, the Cosmic Aviary, and the Cosmic Void. Every map runs its own flock at the same time, so the more places you own, the more feathers pour in at once. Hit a wall and you can rebirth, trading your progress for a permanent boost that makes your next run faster with more muscle behind every merge.

There's plenty going on around the edges too: food drops for short bursts of speed and value, forgiving seven day daily rewards, boosts you can bank and set off when it counts, a Fortune Wheel with surprise birds and crates, and a very serious scientist bird who occasionally asks to take one of your birds "for science." Merge Birds is free to play and fully completable just by playing. Diamonds and one-time packs are there if you want to move faster, but they buy you time, never power you haven't earned.
